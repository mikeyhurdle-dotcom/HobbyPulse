If you only buy one game from this week’s BGG buzz, buy **Clank! Catacombs**.

Why: it lands with mixed groups, teaches faster than most mid-weight dungeon crawlers, and still gives experienced players enough tactical play around deck pacing and pathing.

## The three hot picks that hold up

### 1) Clank! Catacombs — best all-round buy
**Best for:** 3–4 player groups who want adventure without a 30-minute teach.  
**Skip if:** your group hates push-your-luck swings.

**Trade-off:** The tile-reveal randomness creates great stories, but it can punish careful planners when routes stall.

### 2) Finspan — best for low-conflict engine builders
**Best for:** players who liked Wingspan’s pace but want cleaner icon flow.  
**Skip if:** you want heavy player interaction.

**Trade-off:** It’s smooth and table-friendly, but the lower confrontation can make sessions feel multiplayer-solitaire for aggressive groups.

### 3) Arcs — best for groups that enjoy sharp elbows
**Best for:** groups that like negotiation pressure and positional aggression.  
**Skip if:** your table prefers forgiving euro pacing.

**Trade-off:** Brilliant tension, but a weak early tempo read can snowball into a rough game night for newer players.

## What this means for UK buyers

- **If your group is mixed experience:** Clank! Catacombs is the safest buy.
- **If your group values calm optimisation:** Finspan is the lower-friction pick.
- **If your group likes conflict and table talk:** Arcs has the highest ceiling.

## Who should skip this whole hotness wave

Skip these buzzy picks this week if your main constraint is **setup time under 5 minutes** or **strict two-player nights**. You’ll get better value from tighter 2P-first designs than from current hotness darlings tuned for 3–4.

## Bottom line

BGG heat still matters, but only when it matches your table fit. This week, **Clank! Catacombs** is the buy with the best balance of teachability, replay value, and group compatibility.
